## Description (4000 max)

Every sentence hides another one. "Dormitory" is a "dirty room". "Astronomer" is a "moon starer". Anagrams Duel gives you the letters, and you find what's inside.

WRITE A SENTENCE, MAKE AN ANAGRAM
Type any sentence, point the camera at one, or let the app give you one: its own collection, last week's news headlines, or lines from classic authors. The letters become tiles. Tap or drag them into place, add a space between words, and check. Every letter has to be used, and every word has to be real.

MIX YOUR LANGUAGES
Each word is looked up in the dictionaries on your iPhone, in about 40 languages, and shown with the language it was found in. Make an Italian sentence into English words, or mix them freely. Or choose "one language" to keep it strict.

DOES IT MAKE SENSE?
On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the app also tells you whether your anagram means something, and in the games a meaningful anagram scores double. It all happens on your iPhone.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY
• Training: no clock, no pressure. Ask for suggestions when you're stuck.
• Against the clock: 90 seconds to find as many words as you can in the app's sentence. A full anagram scores double.
• Multiplayer: 2 to 4 players on one iPhone. Write a sentence for the next player, or let the app write them all, then pass the phone. Choose the time, rounds, sentence length, languages and scoring.
• Online: challenge a friend, or play someone new through Game Center. Take your turn whenever you like: solve the sentence you got, then send one back. You get a notification when it's your turn.

After every turn, see the other anagrams you could have made.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
Your sentences stay on your iPhone. Online games go through Apple's Game Center.

## Review notes (for Apple)

No sign-in is needed. Online play uses Game Center turn-based matches: two players, each on their own iPhone. To try it with one device, start a game with "Play someone new". The game waits for an opponent while you play your first turn. The meaning check needs Apple Intelligence; without it, that feature is simply off.
