Common questions

Online games say "Sign in to Game Center". Open Settings → Game Center on your iPhone and sign in, then open the app again.

The meaning check is missing. It needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Everything else works without it.

A real word isn't accepted. Words are checked in the dictionaries on your iPhone. If a language seems missing, adding its keyboard (Settings → General → Keyboard) usually adds its dictionary too. Single letters only count when they're words on their own ("I", "a"), and acronyms never count.

When does my opponent play? Whenever they like: online games are turn-based. You get a notification when it's your turn.
