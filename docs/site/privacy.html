Privacy Policy

Anagrams Duel · Last updated 17 November 2026

In short: Anagrams Duel collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

The sentences you write, the anagrams you make, your settings and the list of sentences the app has already given you are kept only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us; we have no server.

Word checks use your iPhone's own dictionaries. When your iPhone has Apple Intelligence, the grammar and meaning checks run on the device too.

Camera

If you choose to read a sentence with the camera, the text is recognized on your iPhone. No image is stored or sent anywhere.

News headlines

If you ask for a sentence from the news, the app downloads public headlines from Google News. This is a plain request for the news feed; it contains nothing about you, though like any web request Google receives your device's IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Online games (Game Center)

Online games are played through Apple's Game Center. Your Game Center nickname and the game itself (the rules, the sentences you send and your answers) are shared with your opponent through Apple. Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive or store it.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
